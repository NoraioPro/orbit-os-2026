      How the rules work

      • Start: oxygen, water, food and power at 70; shielding at 30; crew health at 100. Every reserve caps at 100.
      • Each sol you have 12 points (up to 8 in one system). Output per point: oxygen 2.5, water 2.5, food 2.2, power 2.5. Each point of shielding adds 4 to the shielding stock.
      • Use per sol: oxygen 4, water 3, food 3, power 8. Unused points are wasted. The Net line shows output minus use with no events.
      • Radiation per sol: 12 on Mars, 8 on the Moon, cut by shielding (damage = base × (1 − shielding / 150)).
      • Health: +2 a sol when oxygen, water are above 0 and food and power are at least 25. Food at 0 costs 12 health a sol. Power at 0 costs 4 and halves oxygen, water and food output.
      • Events, forecast before each sol: dust storm (Mars) or long lunar night (Moon) cuts power output; solar flare adds 20 radiation; equipment failure halves one system for one sol.
      • You lose if oxygen or water reaches 0, or if crew health reaches 0. You win by finishing every sol alive.
