      Science by year

        Scoring rules

        • Science per instrument = science value × duty cycle. Instruments that match the objective count 1.5×.
        • Objective factor: 0.4 plus 0.6 × share of needed instrument types aboard.
        • Orbit fit: matched orbit ×1.2, mismatched ×0.8. Data link: Earth orbits 0.9–1.0, Moon orbit 0.7, Mars orbit 0.5.
        • Power: duty = supply ÷ demand (capped at 1). Solar output at Mars is 43% of Earth's.
        • Events each year: solar storm (chance by orbit radiation), instrument anomaly (15%), link gap (more likely on weak links).
        • Score = science × 3 plus a bonus for unspent budget. Launch can fail, depending on vehicle reliability.
