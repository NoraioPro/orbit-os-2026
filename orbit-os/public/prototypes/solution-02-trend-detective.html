Next steps to use real data

  1. Surface temperature: the NASA GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP) gridded anomaly product.
  2. Terrestrial water storage: the GRACE and GRACE Follow-On gravity missions.
  3. Vegetation greenness: MODIS vegetation index products (NDVI and EVI) from Terra and Aqua.
  4. Precipitation: the Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM) IMERG product.
  5. Soil moisture: the SMAP (Soil Moisture Active Passive) mission.
  6. Aerosols: MODIS or VIIRS aerosol optical depth products.
  7. Access: NASA Earthdata Search and Giovanni for downloads and quick plots; NASA POWER for daily meteorology.
  8. Statistics: regrid all series to one grid, remove the seasonal cycle, then correct Mann-Kendall for serial correlation (for example pre-whitening) before trusting p-values.

Sources are named for orientation. Check each product's documentation and license before use.
