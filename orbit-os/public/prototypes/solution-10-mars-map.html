Next steps to use real data

  • Replace the generated terrain with a real digital elevation model from the Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter (MOLA) data on the NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) Geosciences archive.
  • Add high-resolution slope checks from HiRISE and CTX images from the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter, to find hazards MOLA is too coarse to see.
  • Use THEMIS thermal observations (Mars Odyssey) for the temperature layer, and rover weather records (Curiosity REMS) to check the model against measured surface temperatures.
  • Calibrate the slope limit and hazard weights with mission planners and rover drivers, since current values are placeholders.
  • Include a rover or astronaut route-check step using a tested, ground-verified traverse plan before any real use.
