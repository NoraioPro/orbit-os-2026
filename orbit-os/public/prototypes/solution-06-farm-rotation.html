    How the score is built

    • Yield factor = 1 minus heat loss minus drought loss minus waterlogging loss, plus organic matter gain, minus pH loss. Clamped to 0.2 to 1.1.
    • Water saving = 100 x (1 minus demand / max demand), where demand = water use x (1 + warming and drought uplift).
    • Soil health = soil base + organic matter points, minus heat loss on soils with less residue cover.
    • Income = income x yield factor, minus a cost for each kg of fertilizer N.
    • Nitrogen = 100 minus fertilizer N as a share of the maximum. Fertilizer N = need minus legume credit minus usable soil N.

    Using this in a planning season

    1. Before planting, enter the soil test values and the drainage you observed.
    2. Set the climate scenario you want to plan against, such as a warmer, drier season.
    3. Read the ranking and its reasons, then check the trade-off chart.
    4. Shift the priorities to see whether the winner changes. Note any rotation that stays strong across scenarios.
    5. After harvest, record actual yields and update the coefficients for the next season.

    Next steps to use real data

    • Weather and climate: NASA POWER daily temperature and precipitation for the field's location, for history and scenario baselines.
    • Rainfall: NASA and JAXA GPM IMERG precipitation estimates for local rainfall trends.
    • Soil moisture: NASA SMAP soil moisture products to inform drying and irrigation scenarios.
    • Crop condition: MODIS and Landsat or Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 vegetation indices, to check cover and vigor during the season.
    • Soil profile: lab soil tests and local soil survey maps (for example USDA NRCS Web Soil Survey) in place of typed-in values.
    • Calibration: replace the illustrative coefficients with extension trial data or a crop model, and validate against the farmer's yield records.
    Prototype limits: five rotations, one field at a time, and no pest, disease, market or labor factors. Coefficients are illustrative and must be calibrated before any real planting decision.
