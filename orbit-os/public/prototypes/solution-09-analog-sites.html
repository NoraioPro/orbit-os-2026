Next steps to use real data

This prototype embeds placeholder values only. A real version would replace each characteristic with a sourced, dated average for each site and each target, then keep the same ranking math.

  • Moon south pole target: LRO LOLA topography and slope, LRO Diviner surface temperature, and LRO CRaTER radiation measurements for the lunar targets.
  • Mars target: MRO CTX and HiRISE imagery for roughness, MOLA-derived elevation, THEMIS thermal data for temperature range, and CRISM or THEMIS for rock type.
  • Earth analog sites: Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery for surface roughness, ASTER or Copernicus DEM elevation, MODIS land products for aridity proxies, and ASTER spectral data for rock type.
  • Climate and radiation: NASA MERRA-2 or CERES for climate context, with ECMWF ERA5 (not NASA) as an alternative for temperature range.
  • Validation: check the ranking against field reports from published analog studies before presenting it to mission teams.
