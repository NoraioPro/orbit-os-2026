Next steps to use real data

  1. MODIS detections: the MODIS Active Fire product (MCD14 series) from Terra and Aqua, 1 km pixels.
  2. VIIRS detections: the VIIRS 375 m Active Fire product from Suomi NPP and NOAA-20.
  3. Access: NASA FIRMS (Fire Information for Resource Management System) for hot-spot downloads, and Earthdata Search for the full archive.
  4. Matching: pair detections within about 1 km and the same overpass day instead of matching whole-day totals.
  5. Calibration: estimate the factor per region and per season, and report the uncertainty of k.
  6. Filters: apply detection confidence and fire radiative power thresholds before counting, and use day and night detections separately.

Product names are given for orientation. Check each product's documentation for versions and coverage dates.
