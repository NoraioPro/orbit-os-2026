Settings is where ORBIT hands you the controls, and where trust is earned. Everything is stored on your device. Nothing leaves it. You decide the mode: live, pulling from keyless APIs in real time, or offline, running entirely on snapshot. You decide the event source: fourteen thousand freshly harvested events, or seventeen thousand shards. And the language model? Optional. The deterministic kernel does the real work; the planner only helps when you allow it. That is transparency. You are in control, and your data stays yours.
