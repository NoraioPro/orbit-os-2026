Fish hide where no satellite can see. But the conditions they need, temperature, chlorophyll, and depth, are visible from space. SolarFish turns those signals into a habitat forecast for six species: Atlantic mackerel, herring, cod, salmon, bluefin tuna, and sardine. Each has its own preference curves, factor weights, and seasonal zones, feeding an interpretable suitability model that states its uncertainty. This is relative habitat, not a catch prediction, and salmon is tracked for conservation only. You can see where the ocean says life can thrive.
