Ask a question in plain language, and the Analysis page does the rest. Type, Is Svalbard warming? Or, any unusual wildfire activity? In return you get a full, deterministic investigation: a trend line, a significance test, an anomaly scan, and an evidence pack with every source cited. No data-science skills required. And here is what is honest about it: the numbers do not come from an AI's opinion. They come from a deterministic kernel, computed from the data itself. A language model is optional.
