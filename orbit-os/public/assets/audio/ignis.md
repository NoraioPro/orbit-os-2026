When you watch a flame on Earth, it rises. In space, it does not. Published Saffire and LUCI experiments measured flame spread in microgravity at 0.92 to 2.1 millimeters per second, against 1.5 millimeters per second at one-g. IGNIS gathers nineteen NASA PSI investigation profiles and five collections, each traceable to a cited NASA source, kept honestly separate from Earth wildfire data. Understanding how fire behaves in space protects future crews on the Moon and Mars. With IGNIS, you can compare experiments side by side and rank the insights that matter most for fire safety off-world.
