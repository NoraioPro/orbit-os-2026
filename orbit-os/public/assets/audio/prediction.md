Six years of daily weather, gathered across five sites. Six years of daily earthquake counts. The Prediction Panel takes all of it and looks forward, showing you a twelve-month climate forecast and a thirty-day outlook on earthquake rates. You don't see a crystal ball here. You see what is likely, and you see the honesty of it: a band of uncertainty that widens the further out you look. This is statistical extrapolation, plainly labeled, never an official prediction, never a warning. What's ahead becomes clearer, not certain. And that's enough to plan with confidence.
