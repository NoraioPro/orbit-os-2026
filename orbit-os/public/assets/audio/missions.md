The mission is a transfer, not a leap. Three steps, one engine. First, prove the change engine works right here on Earth, against real NASA data — fires, quakes, floods, the signals already streaming down. Then, search Earth itself for the landscape that best mirrors the Moon and Mars — an analog that lets you practice before you commit. Finally, run that same kernel off-world. Nothing is swapped out; the pipeline simply follows the data. What you build today for monitoring one planet already scales to exploring two more. One pipeline. Three worlds. That's the roadmap, and it's already running.
