Eight places on Earth are changing, and you can watch. The SAR Earth Atlas takes San Francisco Bay, Iceland, the Amazon, the Swiss Alps, the Sahel, Oslo, Greenland, and the Mekong Delta, and ranks them by the radar change the ORBIT SAR kernel detects, plotted on a live globe. Instead of a list, you see where the planet's surface is shifting fastest, then click any site to drill in, with live ASF acquisition counts and times for every scene. Straight talk: the change scores come from sample synthetic before-and-after pairs. The acquisition counts and times are real, live ASF.
