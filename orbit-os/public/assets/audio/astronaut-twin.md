Meet the Astronaut Digital Twin. A full-body, three-dimensional crew member rotates before you in space, wrapped in a live telemetry overlay — heart rate, blood oxygen, body temperature, radiation — while an EVA suit console tracks oxygen supply, CO2 scrubber, suit pressure, and battery. One click runs a full suit integrity test. You are no longer staring at a chart; you are looking at a person. Every value you see is simulated sample telemetry. The system never diagnoses; an anomaly only queues for a flight surgeon to review. This is what becomes possible when a living, breathing human is the interface.
